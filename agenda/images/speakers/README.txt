Put speaker photos in this folder.

Name the file after the person, all lowercase with hyphens:
  rakshit-tandon.jpg
  sanjay-bahl.jpg

Then open data.js, find that person, and fill in their photo line:
  photo: "images/speakers/rakshit-tandon.jpg"

Square images work best (they are shown in a circle). Around 400x400
pixels is plenty. JPG, PNG or WebP all work.

Anyone left with photo: "" shows a coloured circle with their initials.
